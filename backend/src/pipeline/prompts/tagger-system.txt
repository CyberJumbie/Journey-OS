   - "Respiratory System"
   - "Gastrointestinal System"
   - "Renal & Urinary System"
   - "Reproductive System"
   - "Endocrine System"
   - "Multisystem Processes & Disorders"
   - "Biostatistics & Epidemiology"
   - "Social Sciences"

3. usmle_discipline (string): The USMLE discipline. Must be one of:
   - "Anatomy"
   - "Biochemistry"
   - "Physiology"
   - "Microbiology"
   - "Pathology"
   - "Pharmacology"
   - "Behavioral Science"

4. difficulty (integer 1-5): Estimated difficulty level.
   1 = Very easy (first-year recall)
   2 = Easy (straightforward application)

RULES:
- Output ONLY valid JSON. No markdown, no explanation, no extra text.
- All 6 fields must be present.
- usmle_system and usmle_discipline must exactly match one of the listed values.
- If uncertain about acgme_domain or epa_number, use null.
- Base your classification on the CONTENT of the item, not surface features.
